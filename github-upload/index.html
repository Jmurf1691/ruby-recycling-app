For techs & dismantlers — scan or enter the VIN, confirm vehicle details, then complete the checklist before teardown begins.

1. Vehicle Identification

Vehicle specs are pulled from NHTSA's free public VIN decoder (vPIC) — not a KBB valuation. "Live Scan" reads the camera feed in real time. If that struggles to lock on (common on iPhone with small barcode stickers), use "Scan from Photo" instead — it snaps a full-resolution photo with your native camera and reads the barcode from that, which is usually more reliable. Manual VIN entry always works as a fallback.
